Self-check26/26

selfcheck.html

Every Adjust knob and every filter, measured through the real pipeline against a synthetic frame with known flat fields and step edges. Each control must be a bit-exact no-op at its rest, move its own metric in the direction its name promises at both ends of its slider, and keep that promise after the preview is scaled to display size. It also measures the sheet's own layout — the value above its track, the centred footer, the centre dot on exactly the centred sliders. It is what caught Highlights and Shadows doing the opposite of their names.
